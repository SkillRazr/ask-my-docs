# Leave policy

New employees get 18 days of paid leave per year. Leave is added monthly, 1.5 days at the end of each month you work.

Interns get 1 day of paid leave for every month of their internship.

Unused leave of up to 10 days carries over to the next year. Anything above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

Apply for leave in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance. Your manager approves it.

Sick leave is separate: up to 8 days a year, and a doctor's note is needed for more than 2 days in a row.
