# Remote work

You can work from home up to 2 days a week once you finish your first month.

Your team lead approves work from home requests. Raise them in the team channel by the previous evening.

On remote days you must be reachable on chat between 10 AM and 5 PM IST, and keep your camera on for stand-ups.

The company pays ₹1,000 a month towards home internet for everyone who works remotely at least one day a week.
